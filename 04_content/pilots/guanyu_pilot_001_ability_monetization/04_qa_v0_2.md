## Cognitive Gain Nodes

1. **Ability is not the product** — ability is raw material; a result is closer to something a buyer can purchase.
2. **Value begins with a costly problem** — the problem must impose real money/time/opportunity/certainty cost.
3. **Capability must be translated into a deliverable result** — abstract skill labels do not specify buyer value.
4. **Payer + trust are separate constraints** — the person suffering the problem may not control budget; the first transaction must reduce buyer risk.
5. **One success is not a system** — repeatable checklists, indicators, templates and review loops turn one-off effort into more repeatable delivery.

- Before takeover: approximately RMB 60k loss over the previous half-year.

### INFERENCE / MODEL
- Buyers respond more directly to solved problems/results than to abstract capability labels.
- Monetization also requires access to a payer/decision-maker and sufficient trust.
- A low-risk first diagnostic/pilot can reduce transaction friction.
- Repeatable processes improve repeatability of delivery; they do not guarantee recurring revenue.
