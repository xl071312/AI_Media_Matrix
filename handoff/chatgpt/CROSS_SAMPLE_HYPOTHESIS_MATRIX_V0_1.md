## Working interpretation

1. No single 'viral formula' is supported.
2. The corpus increasingly supports four behavioral engines: comprehension, save, share, comment.
3. Engagement metrics are associations only until creator baselines and controls improve.
4. The strongest production principle is compositional: reasoning engine + evidence architecture + action architecture + packaging device.
5. H04 remains explicitly deferred; Toutiao article prose is not a spoken Voice Profile source.
