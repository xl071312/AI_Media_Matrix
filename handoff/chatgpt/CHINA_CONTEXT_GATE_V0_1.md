# Guanyu China Context Gate v0.1

Date: 2026-09-13
Owner: ChatGPT
Status: ACTIVE CANDIDATE
Audience: Mainland-China general cognition/knowledge audience

## Purpose

External-news testing exposed a localization failure: a topic can be logically strong yet weak for Guanyu if the audience must first learn unfamiliar foreign companies, platforms, institutions, or cultural context before the real conflict becomes understandable.

For Guanyu, **comprehension burden is part of topic fit**.

## Hard rule

Default priority order:

`China ordinary-person relevance -> familiar domestic scene -> concrete stakes -> contradiction -> news/case evidence`

Do not use an unfamiliar foreign platform/company as the primary hook object for a general-audience video.

### Mandatory FAIL conditions

Reject or reframe before scripting if any is true:

1. **Foreign-entity dependency FAIL** — the viewer must know an unfamiliar foreign company/platform to understand why the topic matters.
2. **Context-explanation tax FAIL** — more than ~1 short sentence of foreign background is required before the first useful cognitive conflict.
3. **No-China-translation FAIL** — the mechanism cannot be mapped to a familiar China-side work, income, consumption, business, education, technology, or family scene without distortion.
4. **Name-first FAIL** — the opening begins with unfamiliar foreign names, companies, platforms, conferences, or institutions.
5. **Imported-problem FAIL** — the issue is important abroad but has no credible relevance to the target Chinese audience.

## Foreign evidence policy

Foreign cases are allowed only when one of these conditions holds:

- the entity is already broadly familiar to the intended audience (for example Apple, Tesla, ChatGPT when relevant);
- the foreign case appears **after** the viewer already understands the mechanism in a China/familiar frame;
- the foreign case is one supporting proof among multiple sources, not the premise the audience must first decode;
- the topic is explicitly about an international event and the international identity itself is the point.

Even then, do not overload the first 15 seconds with proper nouns.

## Domestic-source preference

For current-affairs selection, prefer evidence from:
- Chinese government / regulator releases
- Chinese industry associations
- domestic companies and platforms familiar to ordinary users
- domestic labor, salary, consumption, business, housing, education, technology and family scenarios
- credible Chinese media reporting on directly relevant domestic developments

## Freshness rule

Do not force a weak same-day foreign story merely because it is newer.

For Guanyu current-affairs testing:
- Tier A: same-day domestic high-relevance story
- Tier B: domestic story from the last 72 hours with stronger ordinary-person relevance
- Tier C: older evergreen evidence only if it materially strengthens the mechanism

Relevance outranks timestamp within the short freshness window.

## Integration with Topic Fit Gate

A candidate topic advances only if all are true:

- ordinary Chinese viewer can understand the stakes without prior niche knowledge;
- first sentence can be written without an unfamiliar foreign entity;
- there is a recognizable China-side scene or consequence;
- the topic supports a reusable mechanism, not merely event commentary;
- the hook can deliver a concrete payload in the first sentence.
