# Plain-language corpus notes v0.1

Scope: selected spoken benchmark samples with usable openings / explanation passages. This is not a platform-wide causal law; it is a source bank for Guanyu expression testing.

## B002-003 — result before explanation
Opening: `关于我用8000块，在半年内赚到了130万...`
What matters: no background first. The viewer gets input, time, result immediately.

## B002-010 — direct ordinary-person threat
Opening: `你们知道对钱没有概念有多可怕吗？`
Then: cash used to feel like money leaving the pocket; phone payment makes spending feel weaker; concrete examples such as a 35-yuan drink convert abstract spending into household goods / food comparisons.
What matters: the concept `money perception` is explained through actions and prices, not a definition.

## B002-012 — self-location
Opening asks what level ordinary monthly income is at and whether 5000 yuan ranks high nationally.
What matters: audience understands the task instantly: locate myself on a ladder.

## B002-022 / 767299... — target + error + arithmetic
Opening: `想赚100万，99%的人第一步就选错了。`
Then immediately gives three routes: 100 people x 10k, 1000 x 1k, 10k x 100.
What matters: an abstract business-model problem becomes three school-level multiplication problems.

## B002-025 — blunt counterintuitive sentence
Opening: `你工作上最大的问题，就是效率太高了。`
What matters: the sentence is provocative but fully understandable. The argument later uses haircut / service examples to show why customers also pay for communication, attention, confirmation and process.

## B002-026 — abstract business idea hidden inside a cheap experiment
Core spoken move:
- ordinary person gets an idea and immediately rents, decorates, hires, stocks;
- better approach spends about 100 yuan first;
- use flyers/sample fruit / community entrance;
- ask if people would add WeChat;
- 5 of 10 interested: maybe continue;
- nobody wants it: stop.
Then plain conclusion: `真正厉害的人，从来不看我觉得，他们只看反馈。`
What matters: `market validation` never needs to be named first. The audience learns the concept by watching the action.

## B002-030 — explicit “大白话” style
Opening: `我说真的，我现在看到满地都是商机。`
Then viewer objection: `用AI怎么赚钱啊？`
Then: `我直接用大白话跟你讲，你就永远记住我这句话：你会的，别人不会的，你就能从中间赚到钱。`
Later: `你现在想想，什么东西是你会的别人不会的。`
And a tiny price example: package a tutorial, charge 5 yuan, 100 buyers = 500 yuan.
What matters:
- talks to an imagined commenter;
- uses `我就这么跟你说吧 / 你现在想想 / 你看`;
- turns an abstract information-gap idea into a person who knows vs a person who does not;
- arithmetic is elementary and immediate.

## B002-032 — story before lesson
Opening frames the pain: when someone attacks you verbally, your brain goes blank. Then the speaker tells a concrete meeting story, describes the failed reaction, and only later states the learned rule.
What matters: no definition of `defensive communication`. The listener recognizes the situation first.

## Cross-sample expression findings

1. Concrete subjects dominate: `你 / 我 / 老板 / 客户 / 老师 / 朋友 / 店 / 钱 / 货 / 评论区`.
2. Explanations often move by `为什么 -> 比如 -> 你看 -> 所以`.
3. Abstract idea is usually encoded in a scene, arithmetic, price, or ordinary action.
4. Strong sentences can be grammatically rough but semantically immediate.
5. Speakers repeat important words instead of replacing them with elegant synonyms.
6. They do not try to sound comprehensive. One example often carries one point.
7. Many lines are only 5–15 Chinese characters before a pause/change.
8. The listener rarely needs to learn a term before understanding the point.
9. When a technical term appears, it tends to come after the listener already understands the thing.
10. A useful test: could a shopkeeper, office worker or family member repeat the sentence after hearing it once? If not, simplify again.

## Negative pattern from our failed drafts

Our failed news openings did the opposite:
- introduced multiple domains before one concrete point;
- used consultant words (`标准化`, `权重`, `结构`, `机制`);
- built balanced three-part sentences;
- delayed the usable conclusion;
- treated `像普通人` as a theme rather than using ordinary speech.

## Next test rule

Do not draft a 4–5 minute script yet. For the next external topic:
- first write only 3–6 spoken lines;
- every line must pass one-hearing comprehension;
- no abstract term in first 15 seconds;
- one concrete person/action/money/result appears in line 1 or 2;
- user validates the sound before Logic Engine continues.
