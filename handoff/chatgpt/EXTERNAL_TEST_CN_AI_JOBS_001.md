## Topic source

Use the 2026-09-11 domestic policy/news cluster:
- MIIT released the “人工智能+软件”专项行动实施方案.
- At the related press conference, the China Software Industry Association stated that, as AI changes software production, **pure coding execution ability is becoming less important**, while requirement understanding, architecture design, system integration, quality/safety control and human-AI collaboration are becoming more important.

This is a domestic software-industry example. Do not generalize it into a proven law for every occupation.

## Topic translation

Not: “AI will replace programmers.”

Candidate cognitive question:

**When AI gets stronger, which part of a person’s work becomes cheaper first: execution, or judgment?**

Potential ordinary-person transfer (to be earned later, not asserted as FACT in the hook): many jobs contain both standardized execution and higher-context judgment. The software field can be used as a leading observable example.

>
> 工信部刚发布“人工智能+软件”专项行动方案。随后中国软件行业协会说得很直接：单纯的编码执行能力，权重正在下降；真正更吃重的，反而是理解需求、设计方案、检查结果这些能力。
>
> 你注意，它说的不是“程序员要消失”。
>

>
> 那问题来了：如果你现在的工作，也大量是“别人告诉你怎么做，你负责做完”，接下来真正该补的，到底是什么？
